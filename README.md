Timeline + Proof — a 1024×1024 newspaper front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026 in 19 verified stories across 7 regions: three illustrated hero stories (Belgium's national strike grounding almost all flights, Armenia launching the US-backed TRIPP corridor on Iran's border, US stock futures bouncing after the worst week since April 2025), then a timeline running Asia morning to US evening (Asian market interventions, Nepal–India talks in New Delhi, flash PMIs, the India v New Zealand Test final day, the Ariane 6 launch of 36 Amazon Leo satellites, Monday Night Football), plus a 10-item ALSO ON MONDAY list and a numbered world map with 8 pins. Every story carries its source, time and a certainty tag (CONFIRMED / REPORTED / FORECAST / ONGOING), and a DATE CHECK box lists the other-days' news deliberately left out (COP31 collapse Sun, ATP final Sun, NFL Sunday games, Las Vegas GP Sun, El Chapo capture Fri, India 720/9 Sun). All illustrations are drawn with PIL — no photos, no external images. See [the bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.png` (1024×1024, byte-identical on every run).
